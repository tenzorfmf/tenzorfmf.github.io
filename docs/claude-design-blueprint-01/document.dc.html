Court of Audit report on ŠOU

The Court of Audit of the Republic of Slovenia reviewed ŠOU's operations and financial management. Pages 63–68 describe conduct we think students should be able to read directly, in the source document.

"We also found that ŠOUL commissioned the organisation of several events through Društvo Povezani, but photographic material from these events did not show that ŠOUL was the organiser and payer. The equipment used, and the social media posts about the events, showed only Društvo Povezani's logos — creating the impression that Društvo Povezani, not ŠOUL, had organised and paid for them."
— Court of Audit report on ŠOU, pp. 63–65
