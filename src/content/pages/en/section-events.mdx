Throughout the year Tenzor organises gatherings, talks, and traditional events that
bring together mathematics and physics students. Announcements are in the
**Upcoming events** section, and reports on events already held are under
**Past events**. The **Campaign archive** collects promotional material (flyers and
posters) from past campaigns.
