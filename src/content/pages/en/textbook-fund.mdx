The textbook fund is a project run by the **Tenzor** association to make it easier for students at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics to access textbooks and other study materials.

The fund works simply: we buy back textbooks that students used during their studies and no longer need. This way we expand the book collection at FMF and make sure future generations of students can borrow them for free, instead of having to buy expensive copies.

If you have textbooks you no longer need, we invite you to sell them to the textbook fund. Every so often we organise buyback sessions, but you can also contact us directly at any time to arrange a handover.

*Together we're building a larger and more accessible book collection at FMF!*
