Tenzor is a student list at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics. Our programme rests on
three principles: **transparency** of work and spending, **responsiveness** to the problems
students actually face, and **continuity** — we retain and improve projects that work,
instead of restarting from scratch every year.

Concretely, we stand for:

- publicly published finances, meeting minutes, and decisions of the Student Council;
- a working textbook fund and access to study materials for all programmes;
- better communication about deadlines, timetable changes, and study regulations;
- a place to socialise and study at the faculty, and regular events that bring together
  the mathematics and physics communities at FMF;
- representing student interests in curriculum reforms and in academic-progression
  requirements.

A detailed plan for the current academic year is set out in the **Annual programme**,
and archival material from previous terms is in [Programme 2023/24](/en/programme/programme-2023-24/).
