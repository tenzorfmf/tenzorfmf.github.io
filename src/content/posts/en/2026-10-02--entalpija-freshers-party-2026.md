Tenzor FMF invites you to Entalpija, a free freshers' party at Publika, both
open air and inside. Playing are **Tinex, North, Renex, Timothee, Primkeee**
and **4materasu**, with food and pop-up stands.

The party is organised together with the student groups Možgani MF,
S. Delavne FSD, Pozabljeni, Požar FDV, Delta FRI, Modul FA, Lexia PF,
Proletariart AGRFT and ZLDS FF.
