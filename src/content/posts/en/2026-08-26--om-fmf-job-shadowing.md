OM FMF is looking for six students who would like to spend a day shadowing
staff at a company. The visit is a chance to explore which field interests
you, experience a workplace first-hand and make first contact with a
potential employer.

Placements are expected to take place between October and December 2026, with
some dates possible in the second half of September. Applications will be
considered on a rolling basis until all places are filled.

To take part, contact Eva Šraj (OM FMF) at
[eva.sraj@fmf.uni-lj.si](mailto:eva.sraj@fmf.uni-lj.si).
