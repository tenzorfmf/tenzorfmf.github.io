After being postponed, the first Student Music Evening took place on 4 June
at 17:00 at Kader by Kodeljevo Castle.

Student bands Delta Riff, Milena, Stone Head and Tremor joined Tim Cross on the
line-up. The concert was part of the Month of Youth, organised by the opposition
lists within ŠOU. Admission was free, with no ticket or reservation required.
