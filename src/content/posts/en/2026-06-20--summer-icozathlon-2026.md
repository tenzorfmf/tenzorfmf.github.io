The Summer Ikozatlon brought together twenty events over four weeks,
ranging from card games, quizzes and Sudoku to speedrunning, Minecraft parkour,
darts and physical challenges. Most events were free to enter.

Events took place at the ŠD4 residence in Mestni log and online, before the grand
finale took competitors to Bled for mini-golf. The complete programme appears on
the poster and in the original post.
