Rotor Motor filled Kader's open-air dance floor with five hours of trance, UK
garage, hard house and unexpected remixes. Entalpija × Rotor Motor ran from
19:00 until midnight on 11 June, with free admission throughout.

After midnight, the night continued with the Entalpija Afterparty. The event was
created by a broad group of student collectives with support from ŠOU Ljubljana.
