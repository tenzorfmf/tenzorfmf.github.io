## Library and reading room

The library is in the mathematics building, to the left of the entrance. It
has a large study area and a quiet reading room, open Monday–Thursday
7.30–20.00 and on Fridays until 15.00. Many FMF students also like the
**Central Technical Library** (CTK). It is open until midnight on weekdays, and
during the exam period it also opens until midnight at weekends.

## Kavarna Mafija

The café is next to the mathematics building. With a student meal voucher, at
no extra cost, you can get a tortilla, sandwich, salad, a hot meal or pizza
(the last two from 11.00). It also hosts our **board game nights**.

## Student meal vouchers

If you are enrolling at university for the first time and want student meal
vouchers, first fill in the application, which you can also do online. Keep
the application number printed at the top of the form. Then visit the
subsidised student meals point (SŠP) in person, bringing the application
number, an ID document, proof of enrolment (student record book or original
enrolment certificate) and your phone. The staff there will help you with the
rest.

Once that is done, use a voucher by calling 1808 or through the Prehrana app,
with your phone near the terminal. **Always carry an ID document when you use a
voucher!**

## Tutoring

If you have questions about course material, you can ask the tutors: students
from later years who are happy to help you understand the subject. Their
contacts are in the Tutorstvo e-classroom.

## Notes and study materials

You can find or contribute notes and other study materials on the Tenzor
website under [For students → Notes](/en/for-students/notes/).

## Textbook fund

The fund collects books students no longer need for the library. If you buy a
book from FMF during the year and won't need it next year, you can donate it
and we will add it to the library.

## Website and Discord

Follow the Tenzor website: besides notes, you'll find past and upcoming events
there! Links to the mathematics and physics Discord servers are in the
attached PDF.

## POPR

[POPR](https://popr.uni-lj.si/) is the portal where University of Ljubljana
students sign up for free daily recreation (sports) programmes.

## Student room

A room on the ground floor of the physics building, for studying or hanging
out. It has a sink and a few things for preparing food and drinks.

## Cages/aquariums

Small rooms with a table and a whiteboard on the 4th floor of the mathematics
building, where students can study or discuss course material. They are often
taken, so you can also study at the tables at the end of the 3rd-floor
corridor.

## Tea corners

We maintain two tea corners: in the left wing of the 3rd floor of the
mathematics building and in the student room at physics. Drop by!

## About us

Tenzor is a student organisation at FMF. We organise events, talks, clubs,
parties and more. **If you have questions about the faculty, an idea for an
event or would like to join Tenzor, talk to us at our stand, by email or on
Instagram.**

Good luck!
