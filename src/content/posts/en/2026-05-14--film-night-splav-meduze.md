The Month of Youth film night screened *The Medusa Raft*, the first feature by
Slovenian avant-garde director Karpo Godina.

The film follows two teachers in the 1920s who long for adventure beyond their
small Serbian town. Their routine is disrupted by artists from Belgrade,
Zenitism and an unexpected epidemic. It leaves viewers with a question that
still resonates: what purpose did the avant-garde serve in the Balkans?
